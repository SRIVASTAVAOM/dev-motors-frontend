# Privacy Policy for Dev Motors Expense Manager

**Effective Date**: September 14, 2026  
**Last Updated**: September 14, 2026  

Dev Motors ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our dealership employees, managers, cashiers, and administrators who use the **Dev Motors Expense Manager** mobile application. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Account Information
- **Employee ID & Name**: Used to authenticate you and associate expense claims with your profile.
- **Email Address & Role**: Used for workflow authorization (Employee, Branch Manager, Cashier, Owner) and critical status communications.
- **Branch / Dealership Location**: Used to filter expense queues and maintain branch-level accounts.

### B. Device Permissions & Media
- **Camera Access (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**: Used solely when you take a photograph of an expense receipt, bill, or invoice to attach to a reimbursement claim.
- **Photo/Storage Access (`android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**: Used solely to allow you to select existing receipt photos or PDF invoices from your device gallery.
- **Network Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)**: Used to communicate securely with our encrypted backend servers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information strictly for dealership business operations:
- Processing, approving, auditing, and disbursing employee business expenses.
- Enforcing role-based approval matrices (Manager review, Owner sign-off, Cashier payment).
- Generating financial ledgers, audit trails, and accounting exports for internal record-keeping.

We **DO NOT** sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 3. Data Security & Storage

- **Encryption**: All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using Industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
- **Authentication**: User sessions are authenticated using secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
- **Access Control**: Expense records and employee details are strictly accessible only to authorized personnel based on their assigned role and branch dealership.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Expense records and receipt attachments are retained in accordance with corporate accounting and legal audit regulations. To request account deactivation or review of your stored data, please contact your Dealership Administrator or email us at `support@devmotors.com`.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or legal updates. Any changes will be posted within the application.
